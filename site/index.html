Can a system recognize a seizure as it happens?

Continuous EEG is how clinicians monitor patients at risk of seizures, yet reading it is still largely manual and some seizures show no sign at the bedside. NeuroStream tests whether a model trained on labeled recordings can flag seizure activity within one second, inside a real-time system that keeps working when parts of it fail.

01The problem

Seizures are time-critical, and EEG is hard to watch continuously

Review is retrospective

Trained specialists read EEG, usually after the recording. Hours of signal per patient and a limited number of readers mean findings can arrive late.

Some seizures are silent

Non-convulsive seizures can occur with no visible signs and are recognized only on the EEG.

Time matters

Prolonged seizure activity is harmful and treatable. The sooner it is recognized, the sooner a clinician can act.

What we wanted to find out

Detection
How accurately can a model flag seizure activity in one-second epochs from recordings it has never seen?
Delivery
Can that model run in a streaming architecture that is validated, observable, and does not lose data when a component fails?

How it could help

  • Continuous screening between specialist reviews.
  • Triage: direct reading time to the segments the model flags first.
  • Earlier notification of the care team.

Decision support, not a diagnosis. The system flags; a clinician decides.

02The physiology

What a seizure looks like to an electrode

An EEG electrode sums the activity of millions of neurons beneath it. When those neurons fire independently the contributions cancel. When a population fires together, the sum becomes large and rhythmic. That difference is the signal the model learns.

Illustrative schematic, not anatomy to scale. The model analyzes one recording channel at a time and does not localize a seizure.

  • Seizure-free: neurons fire out of step, so their contributions cancel into a low-amplitude, irregular trace.
  • Seizure: a population fires in synchrony and the activity spreads. Summed at the electrode, it becomes large, rhythmic and regular.

    04The evidence

    Where the decision line sits, and why it matters to a clinician

    Every epoch receives a score between 0 and 1, and the alert fires when the score reaches the decision line. Each setting trades missed seizures against false alarms, so the line is a clinical choice as much as a technical one.

    Data

    500 single-channel EEG recordings from the University of Bonn: 100 recorded during seizures, 400 seizure-free (healthy volunteers and interictal intracranial recordings).

    Validation design

    75 recordings, 15 with seizures, were held out entirely and scored once. Splits are by recording, so no epoch from a test recording appears in training, and the threshold was tuned on separate validation data.

    Precision: alerts that were real

    Shown on the held-out test epochs for illustration. Tuning a threshold on test data would overstate performance, which is why the deployed value was chosen on validation data.

    Prioritize review

    Flagged epochs go to the top of the reading queue, so specialist time goes where it is most likely to matter.

    Transparent by design

    A single score and a visible threshold. Every alert points to the exact one-second epoch a clinician can confirm.

    A second reader

    The score updates every second rather than after the recording is read. The system flags and the clinician decides.

    06Engineering

    Built like clinical software, not a notebook

    The model is only one component. The harder question is whether the whole system behaves predictably when data is malformed, services restart or a dependency is slow.

    1. Ingestion gatewayNestJS, TypeScript. Validates every chunk and publishes to Kafka, keyed by patient.
    3. Message queueApache Kafka (KRaft). Decouples producers from consumers and buffers bursts.
    5. Preprocessing workerPython, NumPy, SciPy. Normalizes, extracts band power, batches dynamically.
    7. Inference servicePyTorch behind gRPC and Protocol Buffers. Returns a score per epoch.
    9. AlertingRaises a structured critical alert with the model version attached.

    Malformed messages leave the main flow through a dead-letter topic, so one bad record never blocks the stream.

    Delivery you can reason about

    At-least-once semantics. Offsets are committed only after inference succeeds, and failures rewind and retry with exponential backoff and jitter.

    Validation that resists leakage

    Splits by recording, a test set scored once, and a threshold tuned on validation data only. Results are reproducible from a seeded training script.

    One preprocessing, two places

    Training and serving import the same preprocessing code, which removes a common source of silent train and serve skew.

    Verified end to end

    Every push builds the full Docker stack in CI and replays held-out EEG through it. This caught three build-breaking bugs that unit tests had missed.

    Typed contracts

    The gateway, worker and inference service agree through a Protocol Buffers schema and validated DTOs, so interface drift fails fast.

    Honest numbers

    The threshold explorer on this page is computed from the real model, and the build fails if it stops matching the training-time metrics.

    07Limits and next steps

    What these results do and do not show

    The Bonn recordings are clean, single-channel and short. Clinical EEG is multichannel, noisy and seizure-sparse, which raises the false-alarm burden, so these figures are not evidence of clinical performance.

    Next: multichannel recordings (CHB-MIT), patient-independent validation, and false alarms per hour as a primary metric.

    08Capabilities

    What this system demonstrates

    NeuroStream takes a clinical question from research, through a validated model, to dependable software. Each capability points to evidence on this page or in the repository.

    Research

    • A testable question, not just a feature
    • Recording-level held-out validation, with the threshold tuned apart from the test set (evidence)
    • Limits stated next to the results (limits)

    AI and models

    • 1D convolutional network trained from scratch on real EEG
    • Waveform and band-power features in one model
    • Score and threshold exposed as a clinical trade-off (explorer)

    Healthcare software

    • Decision support: it flags and never diagnoses
    • Every alert traces to the exact one-second epoch
    • Malformed data is isolated, not allowed to block the stream

    Production engineering

    • Kafka, gRPC and typed contracts (architecture)
    • At-least-once delivery, retries and a dead-letter topic
    • Full stack rebuilt and verified in CI on every push

    Review the work

    Training code, metrics, tests and the CI pipeline are public. Next on the roadmap: multichannel recordings and patient-independent validation.
